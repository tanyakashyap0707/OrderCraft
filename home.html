Features

Built for the shop floor,
not the boardroom

Every feature is designed around how real manufacturing businesses actually operate — fast, offline-tolerant, and practical.

📦

Order Management

Create, track, and manage customer orders with status updates, filtering, and order-to-delivery timelines.

🏭

Production Planning

Monitor which units are in production, set progress milestones, and track completion rates per order.

📋

Bill of Materials

Define the exact materials, quantities, and units required to manufacture each of your products.

🧰

Inventory Control

Get real-time visibility into stock levels. Low stock alerts fire automatically so you never run short.

🚚

Procurement

Create purchase orders, track deliveries from suppliers, and manage inbound material status end-to-end.

💳

Payments & Invoicing

Record payments, generate GST-ready invoices, and monitor outstanding balances with one-click reconciliation.

How It Works

From order to delivery
in four steps

OrderCraft's workflow mirrors the real sequence of a manufacturing job — nothing extra, nothing missing.

1

Receive the Order

Log a new customer order with product, quantity, and expected delivery date.

2

Check Materials & Procure

BOM auto-identifies required materials. If stock is short, raise a purchase order instantly.

3

Start Production

Track units being manufactured. Update progress as your team completes batches.

4

Invoice & Collect

Generate a GST invoice on delivery and record the customer payment to close the cycle.
